Help test VaelNTN games

7 free Android apps in closed testing on Google Play. Each app needs 12 testers for 14 days before it can launch – joining all 7 takes about 5 minutes.

Step 1

Join the tester group (once)

Google only lets group members join the tests. Use the same Google account as on your Android phone. This one group covers all 7 apps.

Join the Google Group
Step 2

Tap "Become a tester" for every app

Being in the group is not enough: Google only counts you after you tap Become a tester on each app's page. Then install it from Google Play.

0 / 7
Step 3

Stay in for 14 days

  • Please don't leave the test or uninstall for at least 14 days.
  • Open each app a few times during those two weeks.
  • Found a bug or have an idea? Post it in the Google Group, or use "Send feedback to the developer" on the app's Google Play page.
